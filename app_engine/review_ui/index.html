What App Review checks

Findings rated critical or high block an app unless the owner waives that exact finding with a reason. Waivers live in the engine's state folder, never in the app, so an app cannot approve itself. Contract checks also run: valid manifest, a launch surface, referenced assets, declared permissions; store releases additionally need version, description, author and categories.
